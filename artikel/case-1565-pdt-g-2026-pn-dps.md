On 8 October 2026, a civil lawsuit was registered at the Denpasar District Court under Case No. 1565/Pdt.G/2026/PN Dps. The plaintiff is S S.R.L., a company based in Milan, Italy, and our client. The defendant is PT FREC, a real estate consulting company in Badung, Bali.

## What the claim covers

The lawsuit is filed as a claim for breach of contract (_wanprestasi_), annulment of the agreement, compensation, and a request for conservatory attachment (_sita jaminan_). The case has been registered with the court.

## The legal basis, in brief

Breach of contract arises when a party does not perform what it promised under an agreement. Under Article 1238 of the Indonesian Civil Code, a debtor is in default once it has been formally put on notice and still fails to perform, or when the agreement itself says that the passing of the agreed time is enough. Article 1243 allows the creditor to claim costs, losses, and interest that result from that failure.

Article 1266 of the Civil Code treats a breach as a condition that ends a reciprocal agreement, but the agreement does not end by itself. The court has to be asked to annul it, which is why annulment is pleaded as part of the claim.

A request for conservatory attachment asks the court to secure the defendant's assets while the case is pending, so that a judgment in the plaintiff's favour, if one is given, can still be enforced. Because Denpasar lies outside Java and Madura, the procedural rule is Article 261 of the RBg.

## What happens next

Before the court examines the merits of a civil claim, the parties are required to attempt mediation under Supreme Court Regulation (PERMA) No. 1 of 2016. If mediation does not produce a settlement, the case proceeds to the exchange of pleadings, evidence, and a judgment. The court will also decide whether to grant the request for conservatory attachment.

We will not comment on the merits while the case is pending.

## For businesses dealing across borders

Foreign companies that contract with Indonesian partners can bring their claims before Indonesian courts, and Indonesian procedure offers tools such as conservatory attachment to protect the outcome. Those tools work best when the agreement is clear about obligations, deadlines, and what counts as a breach, and when correspondence about performance has been kept from the start.
