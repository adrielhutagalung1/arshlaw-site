Instagram Story frames

Adriel and Anasthasia made two Instagram Story frames for the day, and they would love to see your photos in them. Pick one, save it to your phone, and lay it over your own photo.

iPhone

  1. Open the frame you downloaded, tap Share, then Copy.
  2. In Instagram, start a new Story with your photo.
  3. Tap anywhere on the screen and choose Paste.
  4. Pinch the frame until it fills the screen.
  5. Share it to your Story.

Android

  1. Open the frame in your Gallery, tap Share, then Copy.
  2. In Instagram, start a new Story with your photo.
  3. Tap the screen to bring up the keyboard, then tap the frame in the clipboard suggestion.
  4. Pinch the frame until it fills the screen.
  5. Share it to your Story.

No Copy option? In your Story, tap the sticker icon, choose Cutouts, and pick the frame file instead.

If you see an “Add Yours” sticker on their Story, tap it so every photo ends up in one place.
